Business takeaway
  • Cosine compares the mix (direction): what customers spend on. Good for recommenders, share-of-wallet segmentation and text similarity.
  • Euclidean compares absolute position: how much they spend. Use it when volume matters (credit risk, pricing tiers).
  • Cosine = 1 means same proportions, whatever the size. With non-negative data it ranges from 0 to 1.
