Business takeaway
  • Why scale at all? Distance-based models (k-NN, k-means, SVM, PCA, regularised regression) treat a difference of €1 the same as a difference of 1 year. Without scaling, income decides everything.
  • Normalization gives bounded features [0, 1]. Good for image pixels, neural-net inputs and scores that need a fixed range. Very sensitive to outliers.
  • Standardization centres and rescales by spread. The default for most ML pipelines; values are not bounded and it is less sensitive to outliers than min-max, but not immune.
  • Tree models (decision trees, random forests, XGBoost) don't need scaling.
  • Fit the scaler on the training set only, then apply it to test data, otherwise you leak information.
